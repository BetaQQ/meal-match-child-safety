# Meal Match – Child Safety Standards (CSAE Policy)

**App:** Meal Match (Google Play package: `com.MegaSpirits.MealMatch`)
**Developer:** MegaSpirits (Serhat Yokuşoğlu)
**Child safety contact:** syokusoglu1@gmail.com
**Last updated:** November 14, 2026

Meal Match is a dating app for **adults aged 18 and over only**. We have **zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE)** and child sexual abuse material (CSAM). This page describes the standards we apply to prevent, detect and respond to CSAE on Meal Match.

## 1. Prohibited content and conduct
The following are strictly prohibited on Meal Match and lead to immediate and permanent account removal:
- Any child sexual abuse material (CSAM), including real, drawn, edited or AI-generated imagery that sexualizes minors.
- Grooming, sexual solicitation or sexual communication with a minor, or attempts to arrange contact with a minor.
- Sextortion, trafficking or any content that sexualizes, endangers or exploits children.
- Sharing, requesting or linking to any of the above, including through external links or other apps.
- Use of the app by anyone under 18, or adults posing as or presenting themselves as minors.

## 2. Age restriction
- Meal Match is rated for adults and is only available to users who are **18 or older**.
- Users must enter their date of birth during sign-up; accounts with a date of birth under 18 cannot be created (this is enforced by our server-side security rules, not only in the app).
- Any account we learn belongs to a minor is removed.

## 3. In-app reporting
- Every chat has an **Options → Report / Block** menu. Users can report any profile or conversation, including suspected child safety concerns, directly in the app.
- Users can also **block** or **unmatch** anyone at any time; blocked people can no longer see or contact them.
- Child safety concerns can also be reported by email to **syokusoglu1@gmail.com** (subject: "Child Safety Report"). Anyone can use this address, including people who do not use the app.

## 4. Review and enforcement
- All reports are reviewed by our team. Reports concerning child safety are treated with the highest priority.
- Accounts involved in CSAE are **permanently banned**, and their content is removed from the service.
- We preserve relevant information as required by law to support investigations.

## 5. Reporting to authorities
- We comply with all applicable child safety laws.
- Confirmed CSAM and child exploitation is reported to the **National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC)** via the CyberTipline and/or to the relevant regional and national authorities (for example, in Türkiye, to law enforcement and the Information and Communication Technologies Authority – BTK), as required by applicable law.
- We cooperate with law enforcement requests related to child safety.

## 6. Contact
Our designated child safety contact can answer questions about our CSAM prevention practices and compliance:
**syokusoglu1@gmail.com**

# Meal Match – Çocuk Güvenliği Standartları (Türkçe özet)

- Kullanıcılar her sohbetteki **Seçenekler → Şikâyet et / Engelle** menüsünden uygulama içinde bildirimde bulunabilir. Çocuk güvenliğiyle ilgili endişeler **syokusoglu1@gmail.com** adresine de bildirilebilir.
